MAGI

WORD FROM PERSIAN, WORD MAGIC DERIVED FROM IT
IN NT AND OT TRANSLATIONS 200 BC-400 AD, BOTH NEGATIVE AND POSITIVE CONNOTATIONS, MAGI COMING TO BETHLEHEM GOOD, SIMON MAGUS IN ACTS AS THE PICTURE OF THE FIRST HERETIC.

FROM EAST, EITHER BABYLONIA (BEGINNINGS OF ASTRONOMY) OR ZOROASTRIAN FROM PERSIA. INFLUENCES SIMILAR IN EITHER CASE

GROUP OF SAGES, COURT ADVISORS, MAGICIANS, POSSIBLE ROOTS IN 2ND MILLENNIUM PRE-ARYAN INHABITANTS OF PERSIA. IN GREEK AND ROMAN SOURCES AS WELL AS BIBLICAL
VARIOUS CONQUESTS, ALEXANDER, ROMAN, MOSLEM, HAS ESSENTIALLY DESTROYED MOST WRITTEN RECORDS OF ZOROASTER. LIVED TIME OF OT PROPHETS AND FOUNDER OF GREAT RELIGION. BEGINNINGS OF MONOTHEISTIC IDEAS, MUCH OF JEWISH THOUGHT, ANGLES, DEVIL, DUALISM, HEAVEN AND HELL, VERY OBVIOUS IN DEAD SEA MATERIAL INFLUENCE BY THIS RELIGION BY WAY OF THE BABYLONIAN EXILE.

ADVISORS TO ROMAN GOVERNOR'S IN FIRST CENT, SAGES AND MAGICIANS IN BOTH POSITIVE AND NEGATIVE DOCUMENTATION IN 1ST CENT SECULAR LITERATURE.
WORSHIP FIRE, ETERNAL FLAME CENTRAL TO COURTS WHERE ZOROASTRIAN WORSHIP STATE RELIGION, HENCE FIRES OF HEAVENS COUPLED WITH BABYLONIAN DERIVED ASTRONOMY (USE OF 6 FOR TIME AND MEASUREMENT OF CIRCLE BABYLONIAN), STARS AS FIRE, SO BOTH PSEUDO-SCIENCE AND RELIGION GET CAUGHT UP IN THIS STUDY.

IDEA OF 3 REFLECTED BY 3 GIFTS, PROBABLY CELTIC IN ORIGIN FROM CELTIC SACREDNESS OF 3, INFLUENCED CHURCH IN THIS AS WELL AS IDEA OF TRINITY. KINGS FROM ISAIAH 60:3.

AS TIME INVOLVED, POSSIBLE CONJUNCTION OF PLANETS, SUPERNOVA, OR OTHER SUCH, BUT DATING 8-6 BC NOT PRECISELY EXACT, AND MATTHEW IS USING OT PROPHECIES FOR EMPHASIS UPON NON-JEWISH RECOGNITION OF JESUS AS THE MESSIAH, SO HIS INTENT OF TELLING THE GOOD NEWS OF THE GOSPEL INFLUENCES THE WAY HE DEVELOPS STORY, SO PURPOSE IS SHOWING THE WISE, THE GENTILES, AS WORSHIPING THE BABE OVERSHADOWS PRECISE HISTORICAL DOCUMENTATION.
THE THREE NAMES OF THE MAGI, CASPAR, MELCHIOR AND BALTHASAR, ARE FIRST NAMED IN AN IRISH TEXYT OF THE 6TH CENTURY, WITH PROBABLY THE IRISH, WITH THEIR PASSION FOR THE TRIAD_, TO FOUND THE TRADITION OF THREE MAGI.
