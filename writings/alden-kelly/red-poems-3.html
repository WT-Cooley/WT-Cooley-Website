Mother

September 1963

      I think of mother when there's gentle sun,
      I find her too in rain on thirsty lea
      She's part of rest, when last the day is done,
      And sings in brook from mountain to the sea.

      For she is loving warmth and tenderness
      And holds the cup to succor pain of thirst
      She brings our minds in peace to evening rest,
      And joyfully she places others first.

      Mother, stand near,
      As you have in the past:
      Know you are dear,
      To the day I see last

      I too, in living would be as you art,
      Loving, sympathetic, with quiet grace,
      Compassionate, with understanding heart,
      Through you, I feel I've touched the Saviour's face.

Equality

September 1963

      From you, oh lady of the night;
      Who watches clouds gray and white
      Drift langorously past,
      And features no race nor caste,
      But in benevolence shines
      Upon all of mankind
      Whines and prejudices,
      We should take a lesson,

      To this effect:
      Whate'er the creed or sect
      Of all those that we know,
      Rich or poor, high or 1ow
      Race or religious faith:
      We will give heed to each
      With true equality

Alan William Kelly

[~1971]

      Braced upon a time hewn ridge
      Midst lightning and the storm
      Shouting toward the heavens
        to tell Him what He's done

      Of mountains rare
        and grass strewn plains
      Of streams and rivers wild
      Of People and the lesser beast
        for whom He cares the while

      Let howl your Voice
      Unto His ears assail Him with joy.
      And tell Him of the wonderous things
      You've learned since but a boy

      His cannon balls of thunder
      Carroming off the clouds
      Your feet entrenched in His good earth
      Your head turned up and bowed

      Let storm your soul into His heart
      and let Him hear it say
      That you are pleased to see His might
      Upon this Holy day!

Perspective

Alden W. Kelly January 25, 1976

      We can stand on the rim of the mountain
      Or high on the rise of the slope:
      Watch the light break from dawn like the fountain;
      Each morning give rise to new hope.

      Big sky, - radiant glory, gives faith there.
      Each sun livens beauteous earth.
      The warmth, bless'ed 1ight, does make us aware
      Of meaning for life, - Our and own worth!

      Thru the span of the skies He created,
      The limitless scope of above
      You can know that no man must be fated,
      To be out the bounds of His love.

      For the morning is not just for people
      Who glory in breaking of days
      By dawn's light, pray give thought to the steeple
      And He who directs all our ways.

      And the day is a matter of pairing;
      Our work is more, far than for pay,
      By God's grace we can serve Him by caring
      Enough to ease of other man's way.

Top O’ The Heap!!

by Alden W. Kelly, Albuquerque, N.M. 1/1/76

      "There is much fault throughout the land,"
      So shouts the candidate,
      "Just trust yourself in my firm hand,
      Please, no one trust to fate."

      “My vantage in the D.C. place,
      Tells me what's best for you,
      And politics election race,
      Proves office holder true.”

      If what you claim in this you've said,
      "Election is the way",-
      "What of our governmental head
      Nixon put in one day?"

      The Demo speech was terse and bright
      "He does not represent,
      The nation's needs, nor wants, nor right,
      Pray, let's unseat the gent!”

      Yet G.O.P. was otherwise,
      Claimed blessing from above,
      And cited constitution ties
      No race, just party love!

      And what think you? Appointment filled?
      Or do you favor race?
      Just do not let your voice be stilled.
      And please, don't close the case.

      Myself, I say its happenstance,
      And lightning yet select,
      When drummer plays, all join the dance!
      Who knows the next elect?

Sanctuary

February 9, 1976

      Were I to pass this way again, to be
      A man on earth, and sorely need a mate,
      And if my choice were yet completely free,
      It's you I'd have, nor passion yet abate
      Despite the years, now six and thirty gone,
      Our bridge, from youth and pair to being one.

      So full the years, the press of early need
      For labor? What then found my hands for wage?
      To me you left the choice of work, the deed
      To occupy my part of world's stage.
      This was far best the way, you early knew,—
      Fulfilled, as man, in toil I'd long pursue.

      Our link with future, as with mankind past:
      Embodied love, the warmth that you've expressed
      Through birth of those in whom our blood shall last,
      Together reared; prayed all we did was best.
      And comfort found with you when lost our own,
      Our babe, but worse the flow ring adult grown.

      Yes trust and love, acceptance from beyond
      That strengthens union; transcends earthly need!
      No base desire for we are one in bond,
      And face we to our God to probe our deed.
      In truth, what we believe must guide our way,
      As love, which you I bear, completes my day.

Great Love

Alden W. Kelly 1976

      Great Love, all hidden ages span,
      Your son a sacrifice for man.
      Extend my thoughts, present me pure
      Your "table" invitation sure.

      That He was here, knew life and pain,
      The "table" states through broken grain,
      Of bread we eat, recall the Son!
      The suff'ring cross, our sins undone.

      The promise of the cup we sip,
      The blood of life upon our lip,
      Great Love, we know Your Son is Grace,
      Redemption from the sinners' place!

      Drift now my mind, as God desire,
      Know Presence and a Holy fire,
      That from this table all may share,
      Accepting life eternal there!

Fifty Years

February 9, 1990

      The early answer to tying
      A marital knot, is the need
      To rid emotion of sighing.
      Cast off much of urge to be freed.

      The natural love of her being
      So deeply attracts you for years.
      Voice, acts: what her eyes are seeing,
      Events color laughter and tears!

      Though children come of this pairing:
      Tis best not to look far ahead,
      Events are more than just caring;
      We so miss each child who's dead.

      The need for someone beside you.
      To bind wounds and help you impart
      "Joy of life: and welcome the new!"
      Has filled basic wants of my heart!

      Truly: I'm sorry at recall,
      Of people or things in the past.
      My darling can scan through the wall
      With memory and words that stand fast!

      For sure, the trip would be irksome,
      With changes in mate on the way:
      A spouse who lacked where you'd come from.
      Would surely not help make your day!

      Grandsons who tickle your mind so,
      Because of their bootstraps that worked,
      Have made it right from the word, "Go, "
      Self help they dismayed not nor shirked.

      My daughter with classes to lead,
      Not general, but rarer her chase,
      Bringing closer races that need
      A bridge, for religions to pace!

      Son, Ted, tried also as preacher
      Two decades to help those in church.
      Family felt he was a teacher
      We"ll pray that he's near end of search!

      Truly, I"d not change one happen,
      Nor iterate what's gone before,
      With friends I’m napp'n or rapp'n
      Sure, fifty years tally the score!
