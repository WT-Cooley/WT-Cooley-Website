"TWO MARYS" – course description

TWO MARYS: MARY MAGDALEN AND THE VIRGIN MARY THROUGH HISTORY

These two Mary's, out of those named in the New Testament, are very important because of the development of their cults and the veneration of their relics in the later church. Mary Magdalen was very important to the Gnostic churches early in Christian history and later, in the medieval church where many legends of her sojourn in France are still prevalent. The Virgin Mary after the 5th century, became important in art and cult. and today she is by far the prominent Mary. This course will Discuss both Marys, their cults and appearances through history.

MAGDALENA MOUNTAINS (Socorro; S of the village of Magdalena). These take their name from Magdalena Peak, 8,152 ft., on the W side of the range; on the E slope of this mountain are rocks and shrubbery supposedly resembling the profile of Mary Magdalene; the face is said to have become less distinct when the drought of 1950-52 killed many of the shrubs outlining the face. A popular but apocryphal legend is that a group of Mexicans were besieged by Apaches on the mountain, when the face of Mary Magdalene miraculously appeared, frighting the Indians away, while an- other tradition says an early Spanish priest named the mountains in her honor. And equally apocryphal legends says no murder can be committed in the presence of Mary Magdalene's countenance. See also Magdalena Peak
