J. K. Rowling Biography

J.K. Rowing was born in Chipping Sudbury, July 31st, 1965. Her childhood was generally happy. She studied at St. Michael’s Primary School in Gloucestershire, before moving to Chepstow, South Wales at the age of nine.

From an early age, J.K. Rowing had the ambition to be a writer. She often tried her hand at writing, although little came from her early efforts. Age six she wrote a book about a rabbit with measles. After her mother praised her effort, Rowling replied ‘well get it published then.” She admits it was a ‘bit of an odd thing for a child of six to think. I don’t Know where it came from.”

In her own autobiography, she remembers with great fondness, when her good friend Sean became the first person to give her the confidence that one day she would be able to make a very good writer.

“He was also the only person who thought I was bound to be a success at it, which meant much more to me than I ever told him at the time”

After finishing school, her parents encouraged her to study French at the University of Exeter. She slightly regretted choosing French, saying she would have preferred to study English. However, it was her parents wish that she study something “more useful” than English.

After having spent a year in Paris, J.K. Rowling graduated from university and took various jobs in London. One of her favorite jobs was working for Amnesty International; the charity, which campaigns against human rights abuses throughout the world. Amnesty International, is one of the many charities, which J.K. Rowling has generously supported since she attained a newfound wealth.

It was also in December of 1990 that J.K. Rowling lost her mother, who died of Multiple Sclerosis. J.K. Rowing was very close to her might, and she felt the loss deeply. What is Alchemy?

Before getting into how Rowling uses alchemy, I want to first explain where it comes from and what it is.

The whole tradition, including alchemy, comes from Egypt and is known as the Hermetic tradition. It includes not only alchemy, but numerology, Gnosticism and even astrology.

From Egypt there have survived 18 tractates generally known as the corpus Hermeticum. These scriptures of a small, mystic sect which honored the Egyptian God Thoth identified with the Greek god Hermes, under the title Trismegistos’ or “thrice greatest’. They date from the 1st century AD and bring together wisdom from Egypt, Greece and Asia.

All are concerned to declare a divine revelation. Initiation begins with a call to repentance: Then comes personal instruction so the initiate was required to turn away from ignorance, grief, intemperance, sensuality, injustice, avarice, folly, envy, deceit, anger, impetuosity and malice and embrace knowledge of God with joy, self-control, continence, righteousness, generosity, truth, goodness, life and light.

This involved ascetic discipline and self-discipline.

From this the initiate passed to silent meditation in solitude and then the climax was an ecstatic vision of Light, delightful and joyous. This light is described as “the first God” and the knowledge of God is the human’s salvation. By this alone can a soul become good. It is a re-birth and a sense of identity with a creation. Earth, air, fire and water, as well as plants and animals. More, it is to enter into God.

ALCHEMY, wrote Pierre-Jean Fabre in 1636, is not merely an art or science to teach metallic translations, so much as a true and solid science that teaches how to know the center of ail things, which in the divine language is called the Spirit of Life.”

Essential to alchemy is the understanding of “correspondences’ between the material and the spiritual. “As above, so below.”

Thus, the alchemist was seeking under suitable astrological conditions to bring together primary matter and the primary agent (also called secret fire.)

First, to produce a pure mercurial substance, then an incombustible Sulphur and finally the ‘philosopher’s stone’, which had the power of transmuting base metals into gold, but whose discovery was also a necessary part of the realization of the human’s full faculties, and a precondition of the discovery of the panacea, the elixir of life, which would transmute the human body into an incorporate body of light, so securing for the Adept omniscience, omnipotence and the joy of divine love.

Thus, the human being is the true subject of the alchemist’s art. The three principles, Sulpher of his earthly pari, Salt of the intellect and above all, Mercury, the mercury cf the wise, “snowy splendor’, the spark of the soul, the mystical spirit, must be brought together in the Azoth or Philosopher’s Egg set within the Cauldron, which is the human itself.

This is done by the fire of love. than the transmutation of natural into spiritual human may begin.

The first stage of the process is Blackness, the stage of purgation.

The second stage is whiteness, the stage of illumination.

The third stage, or climax, is redness, the color of alchemical gold, and is also called the marriage of Luna and Sol, the human and the divine, the finite with the infinite.

THEMES IN HARRY POTTER

How do you decide if a book is worth reading? in these books we find the answer to four principal questions.

  • “How are we to treat one another?”
  • “What is the answer to the mystery of death?”
  • “When are we to embrace change or flee change?”
  • “What makes a human being good or bad, or even human?”

In the Harry Potter books, it is prejudice that is the problem. In this regard these are not “kids’ books.’ The message of HP is clear: poisonous prejudice is everywhere, and only constant vigilance and resistance to the regime cf ideas (whose government and media authorities foster and codify this hatred) will free us of it.

The HP books encourage readers to take a step back from the world and recognize what an unloving place it is. This step back is the first necessary step to any spiritual lite. The ladder of divine ascent in every revealed tradition requires recognizing the fallen world for what it is.

It is in Order of the phoenix we find out now inbred and insular the leading magical families have become over the generations and how their narrow belief in eugenics or ‘good breeding’ have taken them into the dark lord’s service. Thus, they have divided their world into pure breeds and mud bloods, those who are witches but are not pure. These have muggle, i.e. human, parents. Hermione Granger is one of these. Itis the same thinking that created Nazi camps and soviet gulags.ad Themes in Harry Potier

What all these characters learn is that none are either all good or all bad. The revelation for her is that we readers are the prejudiced ones. Every novel ends with a surprise that all were not what they seemed.

These books are read and re-read because they offer the right answer. that answer is LOVE. prejudice is a failure in love. We recognize in these stories the desire to see what makes us feel best about ourselves even if it means being unkind or dismissive of others.

Harry has to overcome his prejudice of Severus Snape and accept that it was Severus love for Harry’s mother, Lily, that has kept him alive all these years. Harry was able to sacrifice himself in love for his friends because he has the darkness within himself.

That love as the solution to prejudice so dominates the story line of all these books makes it in the tradition of the Inklings, and Christian literature in general.

DEATH AS HER MAIN THEME.

Rowling lost her mother to MS in 1990, the same year she conceived of the Harry Potter books. She said, “In fact, death and bereavement and what death means.. .is one of the central themes of all seven books.”

Every book develops the theme of love trumping death. However, it is in the first book that she has Dumbledore explain about love and death.

“Your mother died to save you. If there is one thing Voldemort cannot understand, it is love. He didn’t realize that love as powerful as your mother’s for you leaves its own mark. Not a scar, no visible sign. . . to have been loved so deeply even the person who loved us is gone, will give us some protection forever. lt is in your very skin. Quirrell, full of hatred, greed and ambition, sharing his soul with Voldemort, could not touch you for this reason. It was agony to touch a person marked by something so good.”

Thus, in the opening book, Philosopher’s Stone, Rowling offers explicit teaching of what death is and the importance of the love of those departed that continues to protect us even in their absence. Especially when this love was a sacrificial love.

This is the major difference between Voldemort and Harry. Voldemort, fearing death pursues personal immortality by putting parts of his Own soul into physical objects so if he dies, ne still continues to live. These Horcruxes have been created by killing people. this separates him from any love of others as he invests in material things to survive. Fleeing human death, Voldemort becomes its nonliving, inhuman incarnation.

In contrast, when given Dumbledore’s choice between what is good and what is easy, Harry always chooses the good even though it means the probable loss of his physical life. He does so at the moment of crises in every book.

The war between the forces of good and evil that defines the final three novels of the series is largely between those who think physical life (existence) and advantage or power are the greatest goods and those who think what gives life meaning (the soul’s ability to love and laugh) is more real and important than just what is visible.

So, Rowling is saying that physical death is not the greatest evil but living a soulless existence in fear of death is the true death of the human spirit and the greater evil.

Thus, Harry in the final book, when Severus Snapes, memories reveal that he must die if Voldemort is to be defeated and that he must die without fighting back. Harry walks into the forbidden Forest and lays down his life voluntarily accompanied by his mother and father.

Thus, Harry understood what Dumbledore had been trying to tell him. The difference between being dragged into battle to face death and walking into the arena with head held high.

To defeat the dark lord Harry has to transcend his ego concerns, even his inherited prejudices and doubts. That is, He has a destiny, but his freewill choices are what will make the difference between his realizing his prophesied end or not.
