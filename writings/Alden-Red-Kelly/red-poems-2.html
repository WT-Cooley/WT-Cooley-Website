Top O' The Heap!

January 1, 1976 
"There is much fault throughout the land,"
So shouts the candidate,
"Just trust yourself in my firm hand,
Please, no one trust to fate."

“My vantage in the D.C. place,
Tells me what's best for you,
And politics election race,
Proves office holder true.”

If what you claim in this you've said,
"Election is the way",-
"What of our governmental head
Nixon put in one day?"

The Demo speech was terse and bright
"He does not represent,
The nation's needs, nor wants, nor right,
Pray, let's unseat the gent!”

Yet G.O.P. was otherwise,
Claimed blessing from above,
And cited constitution ties
No race, just party love!

And what think you? Appointment filled?
Or do you favor race?
Just do not let your voice be stilled.
And please, don't close the case.

Myself, I say its happenstance,
And lightning yet select,
When drummer plays, all join the dance!
Who knows the next elect?

Fifty Years

June 23, 1943 
The early answer to tying
A marital knot, is the need
To rid emotion of sighing.
Cast off much of urge to be freed.

The natural love of her being
So deeply attracts you for years.
Voice, acts: what her eyes are seeing,
Events color laughter and tears!

Though children come of this pairing:
Tis best not to look far ahead,
Events are more than just caring;
We so miss each child who's dead.

The need for someone beside you.
To bind wounds and help you impart
"Joy of life: and welcome the new!"
Has filled basic wants of my heart!

Truly: I'm sorry at recall,
Of people or things in the past.
My darling can scan through the wall
With memory and words that stand fast!

For sure, the trip would be irksome,
With changes in mate on the way:
A spouse who lacked where you'd come from.
Would surely not help make your day!

Grandsons who tickle your mind so,
Because of their bootstraps that worked,
Have made it right from the word, "Go, "
Self help they dismayed not nor shirked.

My daughter with classes to lead,
Not general, but rarer her chase,
Bringing closer races that need
A bridge, for religions to pace!

Son, Ted, tried also as preacher
Two decades to help those in church.
Family felt he was a teacher
We"ll pray that he's near end of search!

Truly, I"d not change one happen,
Nor iterate what's gone before,
With friends I’m napp'n or rapp'n
Sure, fifty years tally the score!

LET THE SPIRIT ENTER

pre-1943 
Sacramental moment, inspire my thoughts again
Relief from earth's intrusion; sins we have as men,
That I may soar, relate to you, and through your son
Realize the promise, eternal life He won.

The grace of His presence, personified in bread,
Redemption; saving cross, we are no longer dead.
His body sacrificed, on high for all mankind,
The path is marked, three nails; His love we all can find.

Mainstream of life, the blood - to quench all believers thirst,
That Father, through His Son, redeemed us who were, cursed,
And by the word of Him, He sent to prove His love
Remember our Lord's death, til He comes from above:

Press in then, to the table, - Bread and wine today
Reach out your hands, cleansed and pure, striving for the way
His son pictured as described in Apostle's word,
By sacrament through faith, eternal life assured!

Perspective

We can stand on the rim of the mountain
Or high on the rise of the slope:
Watch the light break from dawn like the fountain;
Each morning give rise to new hope.

Big sky, - radiant glory, gives faith there.
Each sun livens beauteous earth.
The warmth, bless'ed 1ight, does make us aware
Of meaning for life, - Our and own worth!

Thru the span of the skies He created,
The limitless scope of above
You can know that no man must be fated,
To be out the bounds of His love.

For the morning is not just for people
Who glory in breaking of days
By dawn's light, pray give thought to the steeple
And He who directs all our ways.

And the day is a matter of pairing;
Our work is more, far than for pay,
By God's grace we can serve Him by caring
Enough to ease of other man's way.

LIFT YOUR EYES

December 1955
Now Lift your eyes from greenery
Of shining, ornamented tree;
Above the tinsel, colored light
There's deeper meaning in the night!

Think, if you will, of that first day,
The shepherds, angels come to pray,
Kings of Orient wandered far,
To place gifts under signal star.
At feet of God’s most precious One,
The Baby, Saviour, His own Son.

While Mary, mother, woman blest
With heartfelt Joy held God's Bequest
And Angel voices tidings spread
With wondrous song, relieving dread,
That all at last should know the birth
Of Saviour to men on earth.

Ot Joseph, who was there to state,
The census, and through heavenly fate
Should raise Him boyhood unto man.
We wonder, did he understand?
Or could he sense the sacrifice
Of Him God sent to bring us life?

Yes, lift your eyes from greenery
To hill beyond, and three branched tree, 
The piercing, wretched end in view
For Him who gave His life for you:
That last your soul may climb the height.
It's his birth to recall tonight!

A. W. Kelly
Elder, Monte VistaChristian Church

There's Deeper Meaning in the Night

July 1945 
Now lift your eyes from greenery
Of shining ornamented tree;
Above the tinsel; colored light
There's deeper meaning in the night!

Think, if you will; of that first day,
The shepherds; angels come to pray,
Kings of the East from land afar
Brought gifts by light of signal star
To crib of God's most precious One,
The Baby, Saviour; His own Son.

While Mary; mother, woman blest,
With heartfelt joy held God's Bequest,
And Angel voices tidings spread
With wondrous song, relieving dread,
That all at last should know the birth
Of Saviour to men on earth.

Of Joseph, who was there to state,
The census, and through heavenly fate
Should raise Him boyhood unto man.
We wonder, did he understand?
Or could he sense the sacrifice,
Of Him God sent to bring us life?

Yes, lift your eyes from greenery

That last your soul may span the height.
It's His birth to recall tonight!
